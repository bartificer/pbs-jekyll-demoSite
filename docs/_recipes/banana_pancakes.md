---
title: 3-Ingredient Banana Pancakes
slug: 3-ingredient-banana-pancakes
date: 2026-07-17
description: A quick, easy, and tasty base for a weekend breakfast, or a desert.
serves: 2
ingredients:
  - 1 ripe banana (the riper the better)
  - 2 eggs
  - ¼ tsp sweet cinnamon
  - cooking spray, as needed
serving_suggestions:
  - With maple syrup, to taste, as a desert
  - Rolled with sweet apple sauce inside as a desert
  - Topped with fresh berries and some sugar or syrup of choice as a desert
  - Topped with some greek yogurt and some fresh berries as a breakfast
---
Heat a small pan to a medium heat.

In a bowl, mash the bananna with a fork, and then the back of a spoon until all structure is gone and it becomes as paste.

Add the egg and cinnamon, then whisk into a batter.

Grease the small pan with the cooking spray and add a quarter of the mixture, spread it out as thinly as you can.

Fry for a few minutes on each side until golden brown.

Repeat three times.
