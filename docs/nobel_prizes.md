*"The Nobel Prizes are prestigious international awards established by the will of Swedish inventor Alfred Nobel in 1895 and awarded annually since 1901. They honor outstanding achievements in physics, chemistry, physiology or medicine, literature, peace, and—since 1968—economic sciences. Each laureate receives a gold medal, diploma, and monetary prize, with ceremonies held on December 10th, the anniversary of Nobel's death. Widely regarded as the world's most distinguished recognitions, they celebrate individuals who have advanced human knowledge, promoted peace, and enriched literary culture."* — [Lumo AI](https://proton.me/lumo)
